Night Siege shader pack
=======================

This pack is Photon Shaders by SixthSurge (https://github.com/sixthsurge/photon),
changed for the Night Siege mod. The Photon licence (LICENSE) applies to the whole
pack, including the changes.

What is changed
- include/misc/nightsiege.glsl (new): reads the event the mod is showing
  (uniforms ns_event and ns_strength, set by the mod through Iris).
- Light colours (sun and moon tint), event fog (density, colour, height),
  the eclipse (black moon, rim and corona at the sun), event moons
  (image/ns_moons.png), stars during the eclipse, aurora on aurora nights.

Without the mod (or without an event) the pack looks like Photon.

The pack must not be uploaded to Modrinth, CurseForge or any other platform that
pays creators, and must not be sold (Photon licence).
